Why now

Science has moved to the feed. Its rigor hasn’t.

In June 2026, Nature’s editors wrote that young audiences now get their news mainly from video-first platforms, that science videos there rarely credit their sources, and that platforms should flag claims that haven’t been verified. Lyncei is built for exactly that.

How review works

Two different questions, labeled separately

Checking that a post represents its source is not the same as reproducing the results or confirming the conclusion. Lyncei keeps those apart.

Source checked

Source fidelity

Does the post accurately represent the cited research?

A reviewer compares the post with the study it cites: the finding, the population, the design and the limitations the authors reported.

Critical appraisal available

Critical appraisal

What methodological or statistical limitations affect its interpretation?

A reviewer with relevant expertise explains what the design can and cannot support, and where the evidence is uncertain or contested.

Review makes the assessment visible. It does not certify a finding as true. A post can also show “Review in progress” while an assessment is still underway.

Public to every reader

The content of each review, the author’s responses and every revision made to the post.

The reviewer’s choice

Reviewers can stay anonymous to the public or sign their review with their name.

Planned for the beta

Lyncei will check reviewer credentials privately, kept separate from the identity shown in public.
