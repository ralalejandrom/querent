Why now

Science has moved to the feed. Its rigor hasn’t.

Nature’s editors, June 2026: science now spreads through video feeds, often with no source and no check. Lyncei is built for that gap.

Read the Nature editorial
  • Science videos rarely credit their sourcesEvery reviewable post links its source
  • Creators with undisclosed financial interestsConflicts declared before any review
  • Unverified claims look like verified onesReview labels on every post, never set by likes

How review works

Two different questions, labeled separately

Source checked

Source fidelity

Does the post accurately represent the cited research?

Critical appraisal available

Critical appraisal

What methodological or statistical limitations affect its interpretation?

Review shows what was checked. It doesn’t certify a finding as true.

  • Reviews are publicWith the author’s reply and every revision
  • Signed or anonymousThe reviewer decides, review by review
  • Verified reviewersCredentials checked privately Planned

Fair by design

Reviews judge the work, not the person

Two kinds of review, both built against bias.

Planned

Double-anonymous and private until you choose.
