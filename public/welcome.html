03Human-verifiedWhat we check about every reviewer, and what we will never do.

Human-verified

What “human‑verified” means

  1. The person is real. Institutional email and ORCID confirmed, and identity checked before the first payment.
  2. The expertise is real. Publications and affiliation confirmed. Reviewers only receive manuscripts in topics they have published on.
  3. The review is theirs. Written and signed by the reviewer, never drafted by AI. The manuscript is never shared with AI tools.

No software can prove a text was written without AI. So we verify the person, check the work, and make every review a signed personal commitment. Breaking it means leaving Lyncei.

What we will never do

  • Pay for a verdict. Reviewers are paid for timely, careful work, never for accepting or rejecting.
  • Let AI write the review. Manuscripts are never uploaded to AI tools, and every review is signed by a person.
  • Expose a manuscript. Secure links, no downloads, and each copy marked with the reviewer’s name.
  • Hide a conflict. Reviewers declare conflicts before every assignment.
  • Overload a reviewer. A monthly limit per person keeps every review careful.

Why now

Finding reviewers is the slowest part of science

Editors often send many invitations before one reviewer says yes, and authors wait weeks for a first decision. When one journal paid reviewers for on-time, good-quality reviews, its average time to a first decision fell from 37.7 to 5.5 working days. Read about Biology Open’s model.

Lyncei brings that model to any journal: verified people, paid fairly, reviewing in private.
